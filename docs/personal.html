Astronomy on Tap Talks


I have given two Astronomy on Tap (AoT) talks. The first, was given at UC Santa Barbara in October 2022, titled "1,000 Tools: 10,000 tools", giving a brief history of the tools that enabled astronomy.

The second talk was given at the AoT Gainesville in September 2025, titled "The Problem with Pluto", about the problem with definitions in astronomy, and how it affects the science we conduct. This talk is available on YouTube and a writeup of the same name is available on my substack: The Problem with Pluto.
